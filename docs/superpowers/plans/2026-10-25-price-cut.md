## Global constraints
- Preserve other games. No new dependencies, runtime services, ads or analytics.
- Portrait gameplay at every viewport; letterbox wide screens.
- Sixty active-play seconds, no early loss. Fans travel bottom to top.
- Swipe distance 150 logical units; cooldown 0.6s; all crossed ropes are cut.
- Scissors fade in, travel along the gesture, snip, fade out. Rope halves recoil with synchronized sound.
- Score only admitted fans multiplied by current multiplier. Multi-cut sets at least 2x/3x/4x; a blocked fan resets to 1x without removing points.
- Blocked fans fall dramatically; a random executive laughs.
- Source-backed news context, original artwork and clear fictional adaptation.
- User instruction: no new tests. Use strict production build and direct browser verification instead.

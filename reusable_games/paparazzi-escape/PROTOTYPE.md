# Flash Escape

Reusable paparazzi-evasion game with original cartoon art. Collect three passes and reach the exit in 90 seconds. Camera cones build exposure; three photos loses the run. Planters block both movement and camera sight. Dash recharges in three seconds. Win/loss and immediate restart make a complete loop; repeated attempts provide a short session.

WASD/arrows + Space; touch movement pad + Dash. Landscape and portrait supported. Help, hidden tab and blur pause and clear input. Sharing/copy/save are optional and never change progress. No accounts, friend permissions, referrals, ads or analytics by default. Camera cones are deliberately conservative about cover. Classic and runway themes use code-authored vector characters, no imported photography, recordings or third-party art. A topical edition may name a public figure; its original cartoon and fictional chase require separate editorial review.

Runtime receives public edition.json only. No mutable debugging API; Canvas data attributes expose readonly acceptance evidence. Approval/monetization status lives in the factory, not the game. Preview editions are marked fictional. Automated desktop emulation cannot establish physical-device ergonomics, humor, market interest or revenue.


## Too Much Wing variant

`mechanic: wing-escape` selects the fictional backstage wing game. Three narrow doors and solid equipment require held Space/Tuck to reduce real collision clearance; folding slows movement. Five snags or 60 seconds loses; reach the pickup car after three doors to win. Large original caricature, white feathered wings, jeweled outfit, wing lag/recoil, loose feathers, staged runway/backstage scenery and distinct press/phone/TV crew. No passes or dash cooldown in this variant. Winning images show its character and result. The physical mishaps are fictional; only the outfit/event is the topical reference.

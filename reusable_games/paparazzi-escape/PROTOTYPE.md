# Flash Escape

Original fictional paparazzi-evasion game. Collect three passes and reach the exit in 90 seconds. Camera cones build exposure; three photos loses the run. Planters block both movement and camera sight. Dash recharges in three seconds. Win/loss and immediate restart make a complete loop; repeated attempts provide a short session.

WASD/arrows + Space; touch movement pad + Dash. Landscape and portrait supported. Help, hidden tab and blur pause and clear input. Sharing/copy/save are optional and never change progress. No accounts, friend permissions, referrals, ads or analytics by default. Camera cones are deliberately conservative about cover; original vector characters, no third-party art or celebrity likenesses.

Runtime receives public edition.json only. No mutable debugging API; Canvas data attributes expose readonly acceptance evidence. Approval/monetization status lives in the factory, not the game. Preview editions are marked fictional. Automated desktop emulation cannot establish physical-device ergonomics, humor, market interest or revenue.

# Iron Tap

A rowdy pub management arcade game. Run three 55-second shifts and try to take home $900 in operating profit. Starting cash is working capital. Earn drink sales and tips, then pay for inventory, wages, refunds and damage.

- Walk up to a table to serve or intervene. Tables block movement. Tap the floor or a table to walk there through the aisles. Pints are steady, doubles pay more, and rounds have the highest margin and most heat. Fast service earns tips.
- Watch the heat meters. Step in to calm a heated table or enter a scuffle using your stamina. Time the Finish action in the gold zone to clear the table. A paid ejection clears the table immediately. Bouncers handle fights automatically when ready.
- Hire waitresses to take the oldest orders they can reach before patience or heat rules them out. Bouncers take the most urgent fight they can reach before it ends. Staff skip jobs that are already covered, then recover for 6 seconds after service or 12 seconds after breaking up a fight. The roster shows trips, completed jobs, and why staff are waiting. Staff wages are charged per shift. Restock in cases of 24.
- Move with WASD or the arrow keys, or click or tap a destination. Use Space or the button below the floor to interact, 1–3 for specific drinks, F to step in, G to eject, Z/X to hire staff, R to restock, and P to pause. Enter activates the focused button. The game pauses when backgrounded.
- Profit target, heat, and staffing costs are prototype tuning. Playtest whether it pays to run lean or staff up, and whether doubles and rounds tempt players into taking meaningful risks.

Run `npm ci` and `npm run dev` for local play, or `npm run build` for static output. The factory gallery includes this folder automatically when it builds.

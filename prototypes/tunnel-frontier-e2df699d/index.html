How to play · paused

Protect the core for four waves. Earth is brown, ore is gold, tunnels are blue, dormant nests purple, active nests red, and the core mint. Ore gives +30 credits, clearing a wave gives +25. Opening any dormant nest adds enemies to every remaining wave.

Touch: tap a tile to select and inspect it, adjust with the four arrow buttons, then Confirm tile. The outlined tile and coordinates show exactly what will be affected. Dig from connected tunnel edges, never diagonally. Turrets cost 45 credits, fire automatically within 3.1 tiles and do not block paths. Repair restores 30% for 25 credits. Build and repair only between waves.

Mouse: click a tile to act directly. Keyboard: 1 / D selects Dig, 2 / T selects Turret, N starts a wave, R restarts. Focus the map and use arrows + Enter to select and confirm.

The round pauses while Help is open or portrait guidance is shown. Close Help to resume the same state.

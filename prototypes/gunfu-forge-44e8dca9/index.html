Brawler guide · paused

Clear four rounds. Choose a permanent move fusion after each of the first three rounds; regain two health between rounds.

Desktop: WASD / arrows move. Space / J punches; chain three punches for a knockdown finisher. R / K kicks: slower, longer reach and stronger knockback. Hold either action to repeat.

Touch / mouse: drag the movement pad and tap or hold Punch / Kick. Movement resumes when an attack recovers. Separate fingers can hold movement and an attack together.

Get close: attacks face the nearest standing opponent, but cannot hit across the arena. A gold fist means Punch; a violet boot means Kick. Your feet plant briefly through attack startup and recovery. Whiffs leave you open. A short late input buffer helps link attacks.

Hits stagger enemies and interrupt their windups. Three-hit punch finishers knock them down. Kick creates space; Sweep adds knockdown. Stars mark stun, flattened opponents are down, and rising enemies have brief protection. Pink grunts jab; purple bruisers use slower, harder heavy blows. Red floor arcs warn before attacks. Only two opponents wind up attacks at once.

Lunge and Step-in move before the strike; Backstep retreats after a connected punch. Double adds a close-range follow-up. Slam adds a small impact area; Guard protects only punch startup. Fusions stack for the rest of the run.

Help, portrait guidance, hidden tabs and window blur pause all combat. Restart immediately starts a fresh run.

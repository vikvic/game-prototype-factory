Training guide · paused

Defeat every opponent in four rounds. Choose one permanent fusion after each of the first three rounds. Recover two health and refill your magazine between rounds.

Desktop: WASD or arrows move. Space / J fires; hold to repeat. R / K reloads. Aim automatically follows the nearest enemy.

Touch / mouse: drag the left movement pad. Tap or hold Shoot. Tap Reload. Separate fingers can move and shoot simultaneously.

Shots consume one of six rounds. Reload takes 0.9 seconds; you can move but cannot shoot. Pink triangles warn then lunge. Purple diamonds aim a warning line then fire dodgeable bolts. Contact alone does not hurt.

Fusion order: movement → guard → bullet effects → shot or reload windup. Backdash retreats from aim; Sidestep goes left of aim; Reload Dash follows movement or retreats when still. Pulse happens on refill. Gold ring means brief protection; flashing means a one-second hurt cooldown.

Help, hidden tabs, window blur and portrait guidance pause the simulation. Restart immediately begins a fresh run.

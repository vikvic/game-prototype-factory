How to play · paused

100 seconds. Collect food at a station, pack it on your plate, return HOME and eat to bank points. Only fully eaten portions score. Bronze 200 · Silver 320 · Gold 450.

Keyboard, plus touch inside the plate popup. Enter starts a round. Move with WASD or the arrow keys; walls, counters and the table are solid.

Next to a counter, stand still for 1 second or press E: a ring fills around you and then the plate popup opens (even if your plate is full); you stay put while it is open. Drag the food with a finger or mouse, or use WASD / arrows. Tap the food or press E to place it. R rotates, F auto-places, Esc cancels. After cancelling or serving, move or press E to start the ring again.

After placing, the plate stays open while the food is served (a bar fills on it); you can't move until it is packed, and Esc cancels. At home press E or tap the round EAT button (bottom right) to eat in pickup order; moving pauses eating and partial bites resume. Backspace restarts a fresh 100-second round.

Press H or Esc to close Help and resume.

On a phone or tablet: tap Start, drag on the map to walk (a joystick appears under your finger), stand still at a counter or tap SERVE to open the plate, drag the food and tap it (or Place) to place it, then walk home and tap EAT. Use Full screen for more room.

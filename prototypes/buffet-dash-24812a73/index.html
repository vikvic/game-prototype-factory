How to play · paused

100 seconds. Collect food at a station, pack it, return HOME and Eat to bank points. Only fully eaten portions score. Bronze 200 · Silver 320 · Gold 450.

Tap the room to walk, or use WASD / arrows. Near a station press Q / Serve, then tap a plate cell for its top-left corner. Rotate changes the footprint; Auto-place finds the first fit. Dashed cells preview the fit. Each plate cell is individually selectable.

Stay still to serve. Moving cancels serving and pauses eating; partial bites resume at home. Cancel / Escape stops your current action. Eat / E consumes portions in pickup order. Restart starts a fresh 100-second round.

The round pauses while Help is open or portrait guidance is shown. Close Help to resume the same state.

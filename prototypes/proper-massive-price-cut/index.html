HOW TO PLAY

SMALL SWIPES.
BIG OPENINGS.

Fans walk upward. Executives stretch VIP ropes across their path. Swipe across a rope: scissors zip in, snip it and get out of the way.

One swipe can cut every rope it crosses. Your stroke has 150 units of reach (about a third of the arena width). Long or curved strokes use the same budget. Scissors recharge in 0.6 seconds.

Cut 2 / 3 / 4+ ropes in one swipe for at least 2× / 3× / 4× points per arriving fan. Smaller cuts preserve your multiplier. A blocked fan tumbles out, an executive laughs, and the multiplier resets to 1×. Banked points stay.

Stars come from your points: 25 earns ★, 75 earns ★★, and 150 earns ★★★. Keep scoring after three stars to improve your best.

Only the timer ends the run. You have 60 active-play seconds. Help, pause and leaving the tab freeze the clock. P pauses, H opens help, R restarts; mouse and touch use the same swipe controls.

PostHog receives play actions, scores and campaign tags. No session recordings or persistent player profiles. Turn this off to stop sending new events.

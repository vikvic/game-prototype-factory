How to play
  • Plan phase: pick a mecha and tap a blue-dot tile to move it. You can re-pick the move as often as you like, because it's always measured from where the mecha started the turn. Drag cards onto a mecha on the board (it picks the best slot: destroyed, then spent, then most damaged) or onto a specific part in the panel. Tapping a card, then a part, also works. You get 3⚡ and a hand of 5 each turn.
  • Turn order is always: 1) you plan, 2) mecha fire automatically (each weapon that has ammo hits the nearest kaiju in range), 3) kaiju act.
  • Ammo: every shot uses 1 ammo, hit or miss. A spent weapon stops firing and stays SPENT until you swap in a new arm card.
  • Waves arrive on a timer (shown at the top), whether or not you've cleared the field.
  • Kaiju commit at the start of your turn: 🦖 Rex stomps one marked tile, 🐉 Wyvern breathes fire along 2 tiles, 🪲 Beetle charges in a fixed direction. They don't chase. Step off the red tiles to dodge, or step in to shield a building. A Rex that can't reach its tile whiffs.
  • Your targeting: cyan arrows show what each mecha weapon will shoot when you press FIGHT. Range boxes show the selected mecha's weapons: the dashed box with an L badge is the left arm, the dotted box with an R badge is the right arm. The badge shows the weapon icon. ∅ means spent. Both update as you move.
  • Intents: dashed red line = where a kaiju will move. Red/orange tiles = what it will hit. ⚠5 = incoming damage. 🎯9 = damage your mecha will deal. 💀 = predicted kill.
  • Kaiju head for the city. A mecha within 3 tiles draws their attack (body-block!). You lose if every building falls or every mecha goes down.
  • Parts: a dead arm means no weapon. Dead legs mean no movement. A dead head means 50% accuracy. A dead core means the mecha is down. Hits on a part that's already gone go to the core.

# Orbit Harvest

this is a gunfu game, where like god hand, your moves are dodge and attack at the same time, while you can run around, you have a for example
2 actions you can perform at any time. By default they are shoot and reload. Later on you get skills that you can attach to those buttons, to
for example a dodge back action augment. If the player attaches that to the reload skill, you get to reload and dodge at the same time. The goal of the game
is to action fun that comes from building your own combos of actions over time against enemies in an arena.

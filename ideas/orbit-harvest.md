# Orbit Harvest

Move around a compact arena collecting energy while a slow hazard chases you.
Each pickup increases both your score and the hazard's speed. Survive for sixty
seconds or collect twenty energy cells. Make the risk of taking one more pickup
the central decision. Display controls and allow immediate restart.
